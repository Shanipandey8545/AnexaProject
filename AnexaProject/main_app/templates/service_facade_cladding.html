Facade & Cladding

Durable, weather-sealed, fire-retardant architectural exterior cladding solutions. Our engineered rainscreen skins provide advanced thermal insulation, thermal barrier protection, and high-impact resistance for modern high-rises and commercial towers.

Type 1.1

Aluminium Composite Panel (ACP) Cladding

We fabricate and install Class A2/B1 Fire-Retardant (FR) composite panels. Panels are grooved and routed on precision CNC routers, folded into cassette trays, and fastened to engineered aluminium sub-framing using stainless steel brackets and EPDM gaskets.

  • Non-combustible mineral core preventing fire flame propagation.
  • PVDF fluoropolymer surface coating resisting UV discoloration.
  • Non-staining weather silicone sealant joints for waterproof sealing.
Type 1.2

High-Pressure Laminate (HPL) Cladding

Natural wood-grain, stone, and architectural solid finishes created through thermo-cured phenolic resins under tremendous pressure. Perfect for ventilated facades, villas, and commercial exterior accents that demand warmth and natural textures.

  • Double-hardened acrylic polyurethane layer resisting weather hazards.
  • Concealed mechanical hanging brackets for seamless surface elevations.
  • High graffiti, scratch, and solvent resistance.
Type 1.3 & 1.4

Aluminium & Solid Metal Cladding

2mm to 4mm solid aluminium sheets, pre-anodized plates, and zinc or copper alloy envelopes. Engineered specifically for projects requiring 100% recyclable, monolithic metal panels with no delamination risks under high ambient heat.

  • Stiffened rear ribs to eliminate oil-canning and surface buckling.
  • Custom PVDF wet-spray or architectural powder coatings.
  • Thermal expansion allowances integrated into anchor slots.
Type 1.5 & 1.6

Architectural Louvers & Fluted Panels

Integrated aerofoil, box-section, and corrugated fluted profiles designed for ventilation louvers (HVAC chiller shielding) and aesthetic rhythmic facades. Louver profiles are configured for continuous airflow while blocking water ingress during storms.

  • Tested aerodynamic free-air discharge percentages.
  • Fluted modular profiles with hidden clip-on engagement ribs.
  • Vertical and horizontal alignment configurations.
