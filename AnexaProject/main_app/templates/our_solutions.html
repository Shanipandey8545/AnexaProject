01 Building Envelope System

Curtain Walls

Aluminium-and-glass facade systems for contemporary building envelopes, with attention to grid, mullion/transom coordination, glazing and interfaces.

Mullion & Transom Grid Calculated span profiles handling seismic movement and structural wind pressure.
Thermal & Acoustic Barrier Double glazed acoustic interlayers and EPDM gaskets for weather protection.
Key Interfaces: Floor slab embeds, bracket slot adjusters, perimeter fire-stops, and exterior column wraps.
02 Architectural Surface Articulation

ACP Cladding

Aluminium composite panel cladding for clean architectural elevations, feature bands, parapets, fins and coordinated facade zones.

Sub-Frame Geometry Galvanized steel and aluminium runner frameworks engineered for plane level accuracy.
Panels & Grooving V-groove folding, non-combustible core routing, and UV-stable weather sealants.
Key Interfaces: Parapet copings, masonry anchor tie-backs, window reveal transitions, and sign-band integration.
03 Frameless Elevation

Structural Glazing

Glass-forward facade applications with a focus on jointing, glass setting, sealant interfaces and clean visual lines.

Structural Silicone Adhesion Factory-controlled glass bonding to adapter frames using high-modulus silicones.
Flush Glass Sightlines Uninterrupted reflection aesthetics with uniform weather joint widths.
Key Interfaces: Setting block dead load supports, safety retaining clips, and field water barrier beads.
04 Ventilation & Screening

Louvers

Fixed or adjustable architectural louvers for screening, ventilation-related applications and facade articulation.

Free Area Aerodynamics Engineered pitch angles for MEP heat dispersal, chiller acoustic isolation, and weather protection.
Blade Geometries Box, aerofoil, Z-blade, and drainable profiles custom anodized or powder coated.
Key Interfaces: MEP duct shafts, civil perimeter trims, structural lintels, and bird-screen backings.
05 Entrance Architecture

Canopies

Entrance and architectural canopy solutions integrating framing, glazing/cladding and coordinated fixing details.

Structural Framing Steel Heavy MS/SS cantilevers, tension tie-rods, and spider fittings designed for high load capacity.
Integrated Drainage Internal box gutters and downspout channels concealed within cladding enclosures.
Key Interfaces: Civil RCC beams, chemical stud anchoring, flashing water seals, and drop-off lighting circuits.
06 Perimeter Safety Systems

Handrails

Architectural handrail systems for stairs, balconies, terraces and other required locations, subject to project design and safety requirements.

Impact Resistance & Codes Laminated safety glass options tested against line load and point impact benchmarks.
Mounting Hardware Concealed base shoes, side-mounted spigots, point-fixed rotules, and continuous top caps.
Key Interfaces: Terrace waterproofing membranes, balcony edge curbs, and floor tile expansion joints.
