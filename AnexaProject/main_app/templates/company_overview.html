COMPANY OVERVIEW

ANEXA FACADE SYSTEMS LLP is a facade-focused company offering integrated solutions for contemporary building envelopes. Our service portfolio covers curtain walls, ACP cladding, structural glazing, handrails, louvers and canopies. We aim to bring together technical understanding, disciplined site execution and a professional approach to coordination.

Company ANEXA FACADE SYSTEMS LLP
Tagline Building Better Facades
Business Focus Facade systems, glazing, cladding and associated architectural metalwork
Primary Market Commercial, institutional, hospitality, residential and other built-environment projects
Approach Technical coordination + fabrication + installation + quality-focused execution

What We Stand For

Buildability: We focus on practical details that can be fabricated, installed and maintained effectively.
Coordination: Facade interfaces are treated as a coordinated part of the building rather than an isolated trade.
Quality: Work is approached with attention to materials, workmanship, alignment, joints, sealants and finishing.
Professional delivery: Clear communication, documentation and site discipline are central to our working method.
