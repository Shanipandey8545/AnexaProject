Company Overview

Corporate Structure, Field Heritage, Operational Tenets & Quality Mandate

Incorporated in 2026

ANEXA FACADE SYSTEMS LLP is a specialized, engineering-driven facade enterprise established in 2026. Headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, the firm provides end-to-end building envelope solutions ranging from structural glazing and high-rise curtain walls to architectural metal cladding and monumental glass installations.

While corporate incorporation took place in 2026, ANEXA was built upon decades of cumulative hands-on field experience. Our leadership and core engineering division bring a prolific track record of heading, engineering, and commissioning complex facade packages for leading real estate conglomerates across India—including projects associated with prominent developers such as Oberoi, Runwal, Tata, Raheja, Kanakia, and Kalpataru.

The company bridges the critical gap between conceptual architectural vision and constructible site reality. By integrating structural design calculations, 3D BIM coordination, precision CNC fabrication, and high-altitude rigging teams, we eliminate cross-trade mismatches, eliminate site delays, and deliver building skins that resist extreme weather events, high seismic shifts, and peak wind loads.

Foundational Values

What We Stand For

Four interconnected operating standards guide every design calculation, bracket installation, and sealant joint we deliver.

Buildability First

We engineer practical connection details that can be realistically fabricated, safely rigged at extreme heights, and inspected without difficulty. We avoid overly theoretical designs that cause costly installation bottlenecks and site errors.

Seamless Multi-Trade Coordination

A building facade does not exist in isolation. Our engineering desk coordinates slab edge anchor layouts, structural steel interfaces, HVAC louver openings, and lightning protection systems with civil contractors to guarantee zero on-site spatial clashes.

Uncompromising Material Quality

We rely strictly on certified architectural extrusions (Alloy 6063-T6), high-grade PVDF/powder coatings, heat-soak tested architectural safety glass, and structural silicone sealants from approved manufacturers (Dow Corning, Sika, Bostik).

Disciplined High-Altitude Safety

Facade rigging is an inherently high-risk discipline. Our installation supervisors maintain full compliance with zero-accident safety policies, deploying certified lifting harnesses, secondary rope fall arrest lines, and load-tested hoist apparatus.
