PROJECT DELIVERY CAPABILITIES

Our structured delivery framework translates complex architectural facade designs into safe, fabricated, and installed building envelopes.

STAGE 01

Requirement Review

Review drawings, BOQ, specifications, site conditions, and architectural intent.

  • Site surveys & datum level review
  • BOQ & specification checks
STAGE 02

Technical Coordination

Coordinate dimensions, interfaces, fixing concepts, material transitions, and installation sequence.

  • Bracket & embed alignment
  • Sequencing with civil/MEP teams
STAGE 03

Shop Drawings & Details

Develop coordinated fabrication/installation information as required by project procedures.

  • Mullion & transom profiles
  • Glass cut-lists & panel optimization
STAGE 04

Material & Fabrication

Plan procurement, fabrication, finishing, and inspection against approved requirements.

  • Precision cutting & notch routing
  • Powder coating & anodizing checks
STAGE 05

Site Installation

Execute setting-out, framing, glazing/cladding, and associated works with controlled sequencing.

  • Bracket anchoring & torque check
  • Suction lifters & cradle execution
STAGE 06

Inspection & Handover

Close punch points, complete sealant/finishing requirements, and support documentation/handover.

  • Weatherproofing sealant tooling
  • Final as-built handover package
