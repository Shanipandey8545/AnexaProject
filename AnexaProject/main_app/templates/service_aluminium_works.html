Custom architectural doors, sliding-folding fenestrations, heavy-duty commercial shopfronts, and precision-engineered aluminium framing assemblies.

Aluminium Doors & Windows

High-performance casement, sliding, lift-and-slide, and tilt-and-turn windows engineered using extruded alloy 6063-T6. Features thermal break polyamide strips that prevent condensation and thermal bridging across interior living spaces.

  • Multi-point locking systems ensuring superior security against forced entry.
  • Continuous vulcanized EPDM seals maintaining wind and rainwater exclusion.
  • Durable powder-coating and hard anodized coatings (up to 25 microns).
Type 3.3 & 3.4

Aluminium Louvers & Commercial Shopfronts

Heavy commercial framing profiles engineered for automobile showrooms, luxury brand retail facades, and plant room acoustic louvers. Profiles accommodate large glass spans with minimal framing lines.

  • Reinforced internal steel stiffeners resisting high wind deflections.
  • Concealed overhead door closers and magnetic panic hardware.
  • Bird mesh and insect screens integrated into louver profiles.
Type 3.5

Modular Aluminium Framing Systems

Specialized sub-grid and framing assemblies used to anchor decorative cladding, double-skin facades, solar brise-soleil, and external signage to reinforced concrete superstructures.

  • Slotted bracket connections absorbing thermal expansion and floor shifts.
  • Anti-corrosion stainless steel A2/A4 fasteners and anchoring chemical studs.
  • Pre-machined CNC notched joints for swift site assembly.
