Glazing Systems

Unitized curtain walling, structural silicone glazing, spider glass, and point-fixed assemblies engineered to counter high wind dynamics, thermal movements, and seismic loads.

Type 2.1 & 2.2

Structural & Curtain Wall Glazing

Continuous glass envelopes suspended along building floor slabs. Mullions and transoms absorb live floor deflection while high-strength structural silicone bonds insulated glass units (DGU) with zero exterior metal cap exposure.

  • Two-sided and four-sided structural silicone systems.
  • Double glazed units with Low-E hard and soft coatings for solar control.
  • EPDM pressure gaskets guaranteeing air-tight weather sealing.
Type 2.3 & 2.4

Unitized & Semi-Unitized Curtain Walls

Fully factory-assembled and factory-glazed cassettes hoisted directly onto slab cast-in channels. Eliminates exterior scaffolding needs and allows skyscrapers to achieve complete glass enclosure at a rapid floor-per-week pace.

  • Controlled clean-room structural silicone curing.
  • Interlocking male-female split mullions absorbing building sway.
  • Factory QA testing reducing site human errors.
Type 2.5 & 2.6

Spider & Point-Fixed Glazing

Frameless structural glass panels anchored through precision-drilled countersunk holes and SS-316 stainless steel spider brackets. Suspended using glass fins or stainless steel tension rods to maximize visual transparency.

  • High-grade marine SS 316 castings with articulated routels.
  • Ball-joint routels preventing local stress concentration on glass.
  • Ideal for grand hotel lobbies, showrooms, and glass vestibules.
Type 2.7 & 2.8

Toughened & Laminated High-Safety Glass

Thermally tempered glass 5x stronger than standard annealed glass, combined with multi-layer Polyvinyl Butyral (PVB) or SentryGlas (SGP) interlayers for blast protection, acoustic mitigation, and zero fall-out hazard upon breakage.

  • Heat Soak Tested (HST) to mitigate spontaneous nickel-sulfide (NiS) failures.
  • Acoustic interlayer films providing up to 45 dB sound damping.
  • Conforms strictly to Indian IS 2553 and international ASTM safety codes.
