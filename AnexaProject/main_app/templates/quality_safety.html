01

Quality Planning & Engineering Submittals

Stage 1

Every facade project begins with detailed engineering submittals. We use client-approved 2D fabrication shop drawings, 3D BIM connection sections, structural static calculations, and full-scale physical visual mock-ups (VMU) to freeze all visual reveals, bracket anchors, and corner transitions before mass extrusion.

Signed-off Inspection & Test Plans (ITP)
Approved glass & metal material finish samples
02

Dimensional Survey & Grid Control

Stage 2

Civil structures always exhibit out-of-plumb deviations and slab edge drops. Our optical total-station surveyors establish primary benchmark grids on every floor slab, documenting actual as-built conditions to allow custom bracket adjustments and eliminate forced on-site profile fitting.

Laser plumb verification across multi-story spans
3D datum check before anchor fastener drilling
03

Material Intake & Batch Traceability

Stage 3

Raw material integrity is controlled before items enter the workshop. Extrusions are checked for alloy 6063-T6 composition, PVDF coating thickness (microns), and glass batches are audited for roller wave distortions, bow-and-warp, and Heat Soak Test (HST) certification.

Manufacturer Test Certificates (MTC) logged per heat
Silicone lot verification & expiry tracking
04

Precision Installation Quality

Stage 4

Daily site inspections monitor mullion alignment, expansion joint gaps, SS-304/316 fastener tightening torques, setting block positioning, EPDM gasket continuity, and clean weather silicone tooling to ensure weatherproofing and acoustic integrity.

Calibrated torque wrenches on anchor connections
Non-staining neutral cure weather sealing
05

HSE & Height Safety Coordination

Stage 5

High-altitude facade rigging is inherently critical. All cradles, scaffolding access, spider lifting cranes, and personal protective equipment (PPE) undergo daily toolbox inspections. Working crews operate on dual-rope safety lines with certified fall-arresters.

Daily pre-shift safety briefings & harness audits
Exclusion zone barricading below overhead rigs
06

Systematic Closeout & Defect-Free Handover

Stage 6

Prior to dismantling external scaffolding, we conduct formal joint punch-list walkthroughs with PMC representatives. On-site field water spray hose tests (AAMA 501.2) are executed, and as-built drawings along with warranty dossiers are finalized for handover.

Signed AAMA 501.2 field hose water leak certificate
Comprehensive As-Built CAD dossiers & O&M manuals

On-Site AAMA 501.2 Calibrated Water Spray Testing

Most facade leaks occur around mullion splices, perimeter flashing transitions, and screw penetrations. ANEXA conducts calibrated Monarch Type B-25 water nozzle spray testing at 30–35 PSI pressure directly against installed joints before interior gypsum ceilings are put in place.

30 PSI calibrated water nozzle pressure
Pre-handover internal chamber leak detection
Continuous 5-minute spray per 5-foot joint length
Formal sign-off with project structural engineers

Work-At-Height Protocols

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Discipline

We maintain a culture of personal accountability and strict procedural compliance across all high-altitude work fronts.

Fall Arrest Rigging

Mandatory 100% tie-off using dual-lanyard full-body safety harnesses, shock absorbers, and independent static lifelines.

PPE Compliance

EN-certified chin-strap hard helmets, high-visibility vests, cut-resistant glass handling gloves, and steel-toe safety boots.

Lifting Gear Audits

Third-party tested web slings, D-shackles, glass suction cups (dual vacuum circuits), and motorized hoisting cradles.

Barricading & Debris Catch

Ground-level perimeter catch nets, toe-boards on scaffolding, and marked exclusion zones beneath active rigging vertical drops.
