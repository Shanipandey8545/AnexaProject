Protocol 05

QUALITY, SAFETY & SITE CONTROL

Plan work, access, lifting, and installation activities with safety as a core requirement and quality verified at source.

Field Protocol

Disciplined Site Presence

Every activity at height, from cradle rigging to structural glass setting, follows verified method statements and statutory guidelines.

Project-Specific Quality Notice

Project-specific quality plans, method statements, inspection test plans, safety plans and statutory requirements will be followed as applicable to the awarded scope.

01

Quality Planning

Use approved drawings, material specifications, samples/mock-ups where applicable, inspection points and documented approvals.

02

Dimensional Control

Verify site dimensions and setting-out before fabrication/installation wherever required by the project.

03

Material Control

Maintain traceability/identification and check delivered materials against approved requirements.

04

Installation Quality

Monitor alignment, levels, joints, fasteners, glass setting, sealants, panel interfaces and finishing.

05

Safety Coordination

Plan work at height, access, lifting, tools, barricading and site housekeeping in accordance with applicable project safety rules.

06

Close-Out

Address observations and punch points systematically and support final handover documentation.
