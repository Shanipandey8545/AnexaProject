Service Division 06

Facade Engineering & Execution

Turnkey engineering delivery from initial static calculations and shop drawings to factory fabrication, crane hoisting, calibrated water testing, and lifecycle maintenance.

Phase 6.1 & 6.2

Shop Drawings & Fabrication Plans

Comprehensive 2D CAD and 3D BIM coordination drawings illustrating all structural connections, anchor bracket locations, glass thicknesses, expansion joints, and fabrication cut-lists.

  • Millimeter-accurate extrusion cutting, punching, and assembly sheets.
  • Interface detailing with civil concrete columns and steel girders.
  • Full glass schedule and hardware BOM (Bill of Materials).
Phase 6.3

Facade Engineering & Static Calculations

Detailed structural engineering reports certifying mullion inertia, glass stress deflections, bracket load transmissions, and anchor bolt pull-out capacities in accordance with Indian (IS 875 Part 3) and international building standards.

  • Wind-tunnel simulation and basic peak velocity calculations.
  • Thermal U-value, SHGC, and condensation risk analysis.
  • Safety factors verified for both serviceability and ultimate limit states.
Phase 6.4 & 6.5

Material Procurement & On-Site Rigging

Procurement from certified glass processors and aluminium alloy extruders. On site, trained erection crews utilize electric winches, spider cranes, and dual-line fall arrest ropes to hoist and lock panels in place with laser leveling.

  • Total 3D optical laser survey of civil concrete slab edges before bracket installation.
  • Strict zero-accident site HSE policy and certified lifting gear.
  • Dedicated site quality managers conducting daily stage inspections.
Phase 6.6 & 6.7

Testing, Inspection, Repair & Maintenance

Rigorous quality auditing before project handover, along with ongoing preventive maintenance services. We conduct on-site calibrated field hose water spray testing according to AAMA 501.2 standards to guarantee leak-free facades.

  • On-site calibrated water hose spray testing prior to scaffolding removal.
  • Structural silicone adhesion and deglazing pull-out tests.
  • Glass replacement, re-sealing, and periodic facade maintenance audits.
