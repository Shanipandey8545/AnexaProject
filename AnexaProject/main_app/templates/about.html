01 / Company Overview

INTEGRATED CONTEMPORARY FACADE SOLUTIONS

ANEXA FACADE SYSTEMS LLP is a facade-focused engineering company established in 2026, offering integrated, single-window solutions for contemporary building envelopes. Headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, our operational footprint encompasses commercial, institutional, hospitality, luxury residential, and large-format built-environment developments.

Our service portfolio covers curtain walls, unitized and semi-unitized structural glazing, ACP cladding, HPL cladding, architectural louvers, canopies, skylights, glass partitions, and specialized exterior architectural metalwork. While formally established as an LLP in 2026, the company was structured by seasoned industry practitioners who have spearheaded, engineered, and delivered landmark envelope packages for leading developers across India.

We approach facades not as isolated aesthetic shells, but as active, high-performance structural systems. By uniting practical constructability, 3D laser-aligned site verification, multi-trade interface coordination, and strict quality control at the source, we prevent rework, ensure thermal and acoustic efficiency, and guarantee on-time project completion.

03 / Execution Methodology

OUR INTEGRATED DELIVERY WORKFLOW

From preliminary BOQ assessment to certified water spray testing, our project delivery adheres to an interconnected 4-stage quality governance model.

1

Pre-Engineering & Survey

Total-station 3D optical laser survey of concrete edge drops. Engineering calculations for wind load, thermal movement, and structural anchor bracket design.

2

Off-Site CNC Fabrication

Precision cutting and milling of 6063-T6 extrusions, routing of FR ACP trays, and dust-free structural silicone bonding in controlled curing chambers.

3

Site Rigging & Alignment

Laser-guided bracket alignment, chemical anchor anchoring, and hoisting of unitized cassettes or glass panels using certified cranes and spider winches.

4

Testing & Certified Closeout

Calibrated field hose water testing (AAMA 501.2) before scaffolding removal, bolt torque audits, seal inspection, and snag-free handover.
