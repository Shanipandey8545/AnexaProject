01 / Company Overview

Integrated Contemporary Facade Solutions

ANEXA FACADE SYSTEMS LLP is a facade-focused company offering integrated solutions for contemporary building envelopes. Our service portfolio covers curtain walls, ACP cladding, structural glazing, handrails, louvers and canopies. We aim to bring together technical understanding, disciplined site execution and a professional approach to coordination.

Our 6 Working Principles

01

Safety First

Plan work, access, lifting and installation activities with safety as a core requirement.

02

03

Quality at Source

Control fabrication and installation details early to reduce rework and site delays.

04

Transparent Coordination

Keep interfaces with civil, MEP, structural and other trades clearly coordinated.

05

Client Focus

Understand the project requirement and work toward practical, maintainable and visually consistent results.

06

Continuous Improvement

Capture site learning and improve methods, documentation and execution from project to project.
