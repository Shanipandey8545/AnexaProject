Architectural Glass Works

Custom interior and exterior architectural glass installations engineered for acoustic privacy, extreme crowd-load safety, impact resistance, and maximum optical clarity. Fabricated with high-grade tempered glass, SentryGlas (SGP) laminations, and premium architectural hardware.

Type 4.1

Glass Partitions

Minimalist interior acoustic glass partition systems designed for modern corporate offices, executive boardrooms, and institutional enclosures. Engineered using single or double-glazed 10mm and 12mm toughened acoustic laminated glass, joined via ultra-clear polycarbonate dry-joints or micro-profile slim aluminium perimeter channels to create panoramic transparency.

  • High acoustic mitigation achieving sound transmission loss from 36 dB up to 48 dB Rw.
  • Dry-joint clear polycarbonate clip connectors eliminating messy silicone smears on vision panels.
  • Optional smart switchable privacy glass (PDLC films) transitioning instantly from clear to frosted.
  • Slimline anodized and powder-coated perimeter channels with integrated neoprene acoustic gaskets.
Type 4.2

Frameless & Framed Glass Doors

Heavy-duty architectural swing, sliding, automatic sensor, and center-pivoted glass doors tailored for executive suites, retail storefronts, and commercial entrances. Engineered with CNC-milled edge-polished toughened safety glass and heavy-cycle stainless steel patch hardware to prevent sagging and ensure smooth, controlled closing operation.

  • Heavy-duty hydraulic floor springs tested for 500,000+ opening cycles with dual-speed valve control.
  • Premium Grade 304/316 stainless steel patch fittings, top pivots, and bottom corner locks.
  • Available with integrated electromagnetic drop-bolts, RFID access control, and panic exit hardware.
  • Soft-closing sliding glass door track systems with concealed dampeners preventing slam impacts.
Type 4.3

Glass Railings & Balustrades

Engineered structural glass balustrades designed for balconies, high-rise terraces, internal staircases, and shopping mall atrium parapets. Utilizing continuous heavy-duty aluminium bottom base-shoe profiles or point-supported stainless steel spigots, the assembly holds heavy laminated tempered glass that acts as an independent structural barrier.

  • Engineered to withstand heavy line crowd loads from 1.5 kN/m up to 3.0 kN/m (IS 875 & BS 6180).
  • Continuous heavy base shoes with dry adjustable wedge clamping systems allowing rapid alignment.
  • Laminated safety glass with 1.52mm SentryGlas (SGP) interlayer preventing post-breakage barrier collapse.
  • Slimline top slotted U-channel handrails or side-mounted brackets in SS 304 / SS 316 finish.
Type 4.4

Overhead Glass Canopies

Monumental entrance canopies and cantilevered sheltered walkways designed with structural steel outriggers, high-tensile stainless steel tie-rods, and heavy laminated safety glass. Provides complete weather shelter against monsoon storms while preserving clear upward views and maximizing natural light across building portals.

  • Multi-layer laminated tempered glass with SentryGlas (SGP) core engineered for overhead impact loads.
  • Grade 316 stainless steel tension tie-rods with adjustable fork-end turnbuckles for pitch control.
  • Engineered water drainage slope with concealed perimeter aluminium collection gutters and drop spouts.
  • UV-resistant silicone weather seals maintaining elasticity under direct year-round solar exposure.
Type 4.5

Architectural Glass Skylights

Custom roof-glazing solutions including pyramid skylights, dome features, barrel vaults, and continuous ridge roof-lights. Engineered with thermally broken structural aluminium rafters, condensation drainage gutters, and solar-control double-glazed insulated units that harvest natural daylight while blocking unwanted solar heat gain.

  • Double glazed units: Outer toughened reflective Low-E glass + Argon cavity + Inner laminated safety pane.
  • Internal secondary condensation collection channels draining moisture condensation harmlessly outside.
  • Engineered structural rafter profiles designed to support live maintenance loads and heavy rainwater surges.
  • Custom curb flashings with EPDM vapor barriers guaranteeing a completely watertight roof penetration.
Type 4.6

Commercial Glass Shopfronts

Floor-to-ceiling all-glass shopfronts tailored for luxury retail flagships, shopping mall boutiques, car dealerships, and commercial bank lobbies. Fabricated with extra-clear low-iron glass panels spanning up to 4.5 meters in height, supported by concealed perimeter structural channels and glass stabilizing fins for maximum display visibility.

  • Low-iron (extra-clear) glass delivering true color reproduction with no greenish edge tint.
  • Concealed head and sill aluminium pocket channels creating an open, totally frameless look.
  • Structural glass fin stabilizers providing lateral stiffness against indoor crowd and ventilation pressures.
  • Seamless integration with sliding-folding stacking glass systems and automatic entrance operators.
