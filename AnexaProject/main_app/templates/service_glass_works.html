Architectural Glass Works

Interior and exterior architectural glass installations including frameless acoustic office partitions, laminated glass railings, overhead safety canopies, and crystal-clear shopfronts.

Type 4.1 & 4.2

Glass Partitions & Frameless Doors

Minimalist glass office enclosures using single and double-glazed 10mm or 12mm toughened panels. Connected with clear dry-joint polycarbonates or micro-aluminium channels for seamless panoramic office sightlines.

  • Acoustic insulation ratings up to 48 dB for boardrooms and cabins.
  • Dorma/Geze hydraulic patch fittings and soft-closing floor springs.
  • Smart switchable privacy glass options (PDLC films).
Type 4.3

Glass Railings & Balustrades

Base-shoe aluminium profile railings and spigot-supported glass balustrades for balconies, staircases, and shopping mall parapets. Uses heavy laminated safety glass to resist high horizontal crowd loads.

  • Engineered to withstand 1.5 kN to 3.0 kN/m horizontal crowd loading.
  • Continuous aluminium bottom shoe with dry wedge clamping blocks.
  • Slim stainless steel or aluminium top handrails.
Type 4.4, 4.5 & 4.6

Glass Canopies, Skylights & Shopfronts

Overhead laminated safety glass structures that let in natural sunlight while providing total shelter against rain and storm debris. Designed with structural slope calculations to ensure smooth water drainage.

  • SentryGlas (SGP) rigid interlayers preventing glass sag upon rupture.
  • Integrated hidden drainage channels and gutter collectors.
  • Heat-reflective coating reducing internal solar heat gain.
