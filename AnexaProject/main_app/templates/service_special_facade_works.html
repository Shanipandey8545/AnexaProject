Special Facade Works

Signature architectural features, monumental entrance portals, tensioned cable atriums, kinetic envelopes, and geometrical skylight domes designed for landmark buildings.

Type 5.1 & 5.2

Monumental Canopies & Complex Skylights

Large-span cantilevered structural steel canopies clad with composite metal panels or point-supported structural glass. Skylight structures engineered in barrel-vault, pyramid, and free-form geometries that distribute dead and wind loads safely.

  • Finite element analysis (FEA) checking deflection and live wind loads.
  • Multi-tier internal drainage and condensation troughs.
  • High-adhesion silicone seals with UV-inhibitors.
Type 5.3 & 5.4

Atrium Glazing & Entrance Portals

Multi-story entrance atriums built with glass fins or pretensioned stainless-steel cable truss systems. These grand portals provide unhindered transparency, creating imposing entrance statements for corporate headquarters and convention centres.

  • Suspended full-height laminated glass fins acting as structural mullions.
  • Tensioned high-tensile rod cables maintaining tension equilibrium.
  • Revolving automatic door integration and vestibule air-locks.
Type 5.5 & 5.6

Feature Facades & Architectural Glass Features

Complex 3D facade envelopes using perforated metal screens, dynamic LED illumination channels, curved glass corners, and cantilevered viewing skywalks that require custom engineering.

  • 3D spatial coordinate modeling ensuring zero on-site component clashes.
  • Curved and spherical glass manufacturing with tight optical tolerances.
  • Integrated maintenance cradle tracks and monorail systems.
