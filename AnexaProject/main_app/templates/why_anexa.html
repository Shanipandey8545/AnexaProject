Section 06

Engineering That Protects Your Schedule

In modern construction, the building facade accounts for 15-25% of total project expenditure and represents the primary critical path item before interior fit-outs can begin. ANEXA delivers the structural acumen, fabrication bandwidth, and disciplined site protocols required to seal building skins on schedule without commercial disputes.

01 Core Discipline

Pure Facade Specialization

Unlike general civil contractors who sub-let facade trades to fragmented labour gangs, ANEXA lives and breathes building envelopes. Every resource—from our static engineers to our lifting riggers—is dedicated entirely to glazing, cladding, and architectural metalwork.

  • In-depth expertise in mullion wind-load statics (IS 875 Part 3).
  • Deep knowledge of glass performance (SHGC, U-Value, VLT).
Envelopes & Metalwork
02 Turnkey Scope

Single-Window Coordination

We resolve multi-trade finger-pointing. By taking unified ownership over the structural glazing, ACP/HPL cladding, entrance canopies, and architectural louvers, our engineering desk acts as the sole, accountable coordinator for the complete outer skin.

  • Single point of contact for PMCs, architects & quantity surveyors.
  • Seamless flashing joints between curtain walls and cladding trays.
Turnkey Envelope Scope
03 Buildability Check

Technical + Site Realism

Drawings that look gorgeous on CAD often fail on actual concrete slabs. We bridge this gap by evaluating casting tolerances, slab deflections, and installation sequences long before aluminum profiles are extruded or glass orders are locked.

  • Comprehensive 3D laser scans and civil interface surveys.
  • Thermal expansion allowances calculated for extreme Indian summers.
Constructability Checks
04 Fabrication Ready

Practical Detailing

Complex engineering is useless if workshop technicians cannot interpret it. Our fabrication drawings provide millimeter-explicit notch cut-lists, fastener torque charts, and gasket insertion paths that leave zero ambiguity on the factory floor.

  • Automated cutting optimization minimizing aluminum scrap loss.
  • Standardized bracket geometry for rapid on-site assembly.
Clear Fabrication Data
05 Transparency

Professional Communication

We prioritize documentation discipline. From detailed Requests for Information (RFIs) and material test certificates (MTCs) to weekly site progress tracking and anchor pull-out test logs, your project management team always has complete visibility.

  • Formal submittals with manufacturer warranty backings.
  • Proactive reporting preventing commercial scope creep.
Transparent Reporting
06 Zero Defect

Quality-Oriented Execution

Quality control happens at the source, not during final handover. We enforce dust-free structural silicone bonding, calibrated torque wrenches on every bracket bolt, and mandatory on-site water spray hose tests according to AAMA 501.2 protocols.

  • Calibrated hose spray testing before scaffold dismantling.
  • Methodical snag-free punch-list close-outs.
Defect-Free Handover
